How to Convert JPG & PNG Images to PDF

Photos of documents, screenshots and scans are easier to share as one PDF. Here is how to make it in a minute.

Last updated: 2026-11-05

Steps

  1. Open the Image to PDF converter.
  2. Select your JPG, PNG or WebP images, or drag them in.
  3. Use the arrows to set the page order.
  4. Pick a page size: A4, US Letter, or fit to each image.
  5. Click Create PDF and save the file.

Choosing a page size

A4 or Letter suits documents you may print. Fit to each image makes every page exactly as large as its picture, which is best for screenshots or artwork.

Tips for better results

  • Photograph documents flat in good light to avoid shadows and skewed text.
  • Check the order before converting. It is easier than fixing it later.
  • Keep photos reasonable in size. A PDF built from several full-size phone photos can be large.

Making the PDF smaller

If the finished PDF is too large to send, use Compress PDF. For multi-part documents, you can also merge PDFs created in separate batches.

Privacy

Images are converted in your browser and are not uploaded to EditPDF.
