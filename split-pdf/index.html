Split PDF & Extract Pages — Free

Pull the pages you need out of a PDF and save them as a new file. Type the page numbers and the tool does the rest in your browser.

Download

🔒 Your files stay on your device — they are processed in your browser and never uploaded. Processing may take a moment for large documents.

How to use Split PDF

  1. Choose a PDF. Select or drag in the document you want to split.
  2. Enter the pages. Type page numbers and ranges, for example 1,3-5 to keep pages 1, 3, 4 and 5.
  3. Download the new PDF. Click Extract pages. The new, shorter PDF downloads immediately.

About Split PDF

Splitting a PDF is helpful when you only need part of a long document: a single invoice from a statement bundle, one chapter from a manual, or the signature page of a contract.

Good to know: the tool creates one new PDF from the pages you list. To get several separate files, run it once for each set of pages. Password-protected PDFs cannot be processed.

Split PDF — frequently asked questions

How do I extract pages from a PDF?

Upload the file, type the pages you want, such as 2-4, and click Extract pages. A new PDF containing only those pages is downloaded.

How do I write the page range?

Separate items with commas and use a hyphen for ranges: 1,3-5,8 keeps pages 1, 3, 4, 5 and 8. Page numbers start at 1.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied directly, so there is no quality loss.

Can I split a PDF into many files at once?

Not in one step. Run the tool once for each group of pages you need.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
