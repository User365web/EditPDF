Merge PDF Files Online — Free

Combine two or more PDFs into one document in the order you choose. Nothing is uploaded, because the merge happens in your browser.

Download

🔒 Your files stay on your device — they are processed in your browser and never uploaded. Processing may take a moment for large documents.

How to use Merge PDF

  1. Add two or more PDFs. Select your files or drag them into the box. Add more at any time.
  2. Arrange the order. Use the arrows to move files up or down. The top file comes first.
  3. Merge and download. Click Merge PDFs. Your combined file downloads automatically.

About Merge PDF

Merging PDFs saves you from sending many separate attachments. Typical uses are job applications (CV plus certificates), invoices for one month, or chapters of a report prepared by different people.

Good to know: password-protected or damaged PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password in the program that created the file first. Very large files can take a while on older devices.

Merge PDF — frequently asked questions

How do I merge PDF files for free?

Add your PDFs, put them in the order you want with the arrows, and click Merge PDFs. The combined file downloads to your device.

Is the text still selectable after merging?

Yes. Pages are copied as they are, not converted to images.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Not directly. Remove the password using the original program, then merge the unlocked files.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Merging runs in your browser with a JavaScript library, so your files stay on your device.

Is there a limit on the number of files?

There is no fixed limit. The practical limit is your device's memory.
