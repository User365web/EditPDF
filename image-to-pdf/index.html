How to use Image to PDF

  1. Add your images. Select JPG, PNG or WebP files, or drag them in. You can add more than one batch.
  2. Set order and page size. Use the arrows to reorder pages, then choose A4, US Letter or fit-to-image.
  3. Create and download. Click Create PDF. Your document is built on your device and downloads straight away.

About Image to PDF

Turning pictures into a PDF is useful for scanned receipts, signed forms photographed with a phone, school assignments and portfolios. A PDF keeps your pages in a fixed order and opens the same way on every device.

Good to know: WebP images are converted to JPEG before they are placed in the PDF, and transparent areas become white. Very large photos can make large PDFs; use the Compress PDF tool afterwards if you need a smaller file.

Image to PDF — frequently asked questions

Which image formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP.

Can I put several images into one PDF?

Yes. Add as many images as your device can handle and reorder them with the arrows. Each image becomes its own page.

Will my images be uploaded?

No. They are read by your browser and the PDF is created locally.

What does 'fit page to each image' do?

Each PDF page is sized to match its image, with no margins. Choose A4 or Letter if you want standard paper-size pages.

How can I make the PDF smaller?

Resize large photos before converting, or run the finished file through the Compress PDF tool.
